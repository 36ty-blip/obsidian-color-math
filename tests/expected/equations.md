## 1. Triple nested power + trigonometric chain rule

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{\left[\sin\left(f(g(y))^n\right)\right]^m}
=
\textcolor{#e0af68}{m}\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{\left[\sin\left(f(g(y))^n\right)\right]^{m-1}}
\cdot
\textcolor{#bb9af7}{\cos\left(f(g(y))^n\right)}
\cdot
\textcolor{#e0af68}{n} \textcolor{#7aa2f7}{f(g(y))^{n-1}}
\cdot
\textcolor{#bb9af7}{f'(g(y))}\textcolor{#9ece6a}{g'(y)}\textcolor{#9ece6a}{y'}
$$

## 2. Logarithm of quotient with nested powers

$$
\frac{d}{dx}
\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{\ln\left(\frac{f(y)^n}{g(y)^m}\right)}
=
\textcolor{#bb9af7}{\frac{
\textcolor{#e0af68}{n} \textcolor{#7aa2f7}{f(y)^{n-1}}\textcolor{#bb9af7}{f'(y)}\textcolor{#9ece6a}{y'}\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{g(y)^m}
-
\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{f(y)^n} \textcolor{#e0af68}{m} \textcolor{#7aa2f7}{g(y)^{m-1}}\textcolor{#bb9af7}{g'(y)}\textcolor{#9ece6a}{y'}
}{
\textcolor{#7aa2f7}{f(y)^n} \textcolor{#7aa2f7}{g(y)^m}
}}
$$
